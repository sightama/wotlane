War of the Monsters on Mac: play online with friends
====================================================

This is the PC version of War of the Monsters (PS2) from the wotlane project, for Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). It does not
contain the game itself: you need your own copy of the game as an ISO of the USA disc (SCUS-97197). Please keep this package private;
it is for friends who own the game.


Install (once)
--------------
1. Unzip the download and drag "War of the Monsters" into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app (it isn't from the App Store). Press "Done", then open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and press "Open Anyway" next to "War of the Monsters". Confirm with your password.
3. The app asks for your War of the Monsters (USA) .iso: pick it. Don't move or rename the ISO afterwards (if you do, the app asks again).


Join a friend's game
--------------------
1. Open "War of the Monsters" and press "Join a game".
2. Type your name, pick your monster, then type the 4-letter code your friend sent you.
3. The game starts by itself when your friend is ready.


Host a game
-----------
1. Open "War of the Monsters" and press "Host a game".
2. Type your name, pick your monster, the stage and how many players.
3. The app shows your 4-letter code and copies it: paste it to your friends. The game starts by itself when they have all joined.
Nothing to set up on your router: the game runs through a shared online server.


Play alone
----------
Press "Play offline".


If something goes wrong
-----------------------
The app says what's wrong in plain words. The usual ones:
- "There is no game ...": the code is wrong or that game is over. Ask your friend for a new code.
- "a different version": everyone needs the same download (compare BUILD.txt).
- Anything else: send your friend the files in your home folder under Library/Application Support/wotlane/logs
  (in Finder: Go > Go to Folder, then type ~/Library/Application Support/wotlane/logs).
