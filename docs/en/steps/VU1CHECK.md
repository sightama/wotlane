# VU1 x native check

State, leads and fixes of the check. Index in [`NEXT_STEPS.md`](../NEXT_STEPS.md); the method (per-chain collection, back faces, output format) is in [`NATIVE.md`](NATIVE.md), "Runtime: checagem VU1 x nativo" (Runtime: VU1 x native check). Disassembled microcode in `build/vu1/` (`tools/vu1dis.py`; game code, outside the repo).

<details>
<summary>How it works</summary>

- `WOTM_VU1CHECK=<s>` (runtime, `wotm_vu1check.inc`): every `<s>` s, in a fight frame, the VU1 executes the frame's chain. Each XGKICK triangle is matched (1 px) with the native one of the same object (`polyPkt`); each particle quad, with the one of its `fx[i]`. A geometry tie is broken by color.
- It compares geometry, texture (TEX0) and the shape of the particles. On the matched pairs, also the attributes (line `atributos:` (attributes) and up to 8 objects `atributos pkt`):
  - `uv difere` (uv differs; > 1 texel; `volta inteira` (full wrap) does not count), `cor difere` (color differs; RGB > 4, the color the native side draws), `alfa difere` (alpha differs; with ABE on both), `ABE difere`, `ALPHA difere`, `TFX/TCC fora do padrao` (TFX/TCC off the standard);
  - `neblina N (FGE difere, F difere)` (fog N (FGE differs, F differs)): F of XYZF2 against `Scene::fogF`.
- **Since 2026-10-18** (method in [`NATIVE.md`](NATIVE.md)): line `particulas cor` (particle color; vertex color and alpha of the matched particles, by type), line `linhas` (lines; `FX_LINE`, segment by segment), fans as triangles and, only in `--vu1cmp` with images, line `texels` (the texture of each TEX0 read from the GS VRAM at draw time against the one the native side decodes; diagnostics `WOTM_VU1CHECK_TEXELS=<id>` and `WOTM_VU1CHECK_TEXREC=1`).
- **Limits of the method:** VU1 points are not compared (none in the corpus); for particles, ABE, ALPHA and UV are left out (only the vertex color and alpha); texels only in the replay with images. The PATH2/PATH3 HUD only enters through the inventory (count and box), without triangle comparison; the `hier` one is compared like the other objects. The fog was checked separately (mask from `WOTM_FOGSPLIT=2` on METRO: FA −107.9, FB 326,571, color 45/33/20; it matters from W ≈ 900 on).
- Counted separately: `repetidos` (repeated; passes of the projected shadow), `so pela sombra` (only from the shadow; tbp 3e60), `em textura (sombra)` (in texture (shadow); FRAME outside the screen buffers 0x46/0x8C), `costas pelo recorte` (back faces by clipping).
- **Inventory (2026-10-16, `inv` lines):** what each side draws without a pair on the other, by owner, on any screen (the sampled frame goes from one `hier(0,0)` to the next, from any caller: `rtMain` in the fight, `userintMain` in the menus). On the reference side go the VU1 triangles and SPRITEs with no native object or `fx[]` (by MSCAL origin) and **all the PATH2 (DIRECT) and PATH3 (GIF channel) primitives**, grouped by path, type, texture, FRAME and ABE, with an on-screen box in pixels (`TELA INTEIRA` (full screen) = fade/clear; `em textura` (in texture) = FRAME off screen; `src` = the packet's origin in RAM, only in live PATH2). On the native side, the objects (including sky and icons) whose `polyPkt` no VU1 triangle touched, with `alfa max` (max alpha; 0 = the native side "draws" invisibly). Summary: `inv g=N so a referencia: G grupos, P primitivos (...) | so o nativo: K objetos, T tris na tela | objetos nos dois lados M` (only the reference: G groups, P primitives (...) | only the native: K objects, T tris on screen | objects on both sides M). **Names (day 2, 2026-10-16):** the texture comes out as `tex <id>` (index in `texInfo`, the number the native side uses; from the DBP of the upload packet), the origin in RAM as `psBorderDma+0x10` (table `wotm::symtab` generated in `wotm_syms.h` by `tools/native_syms.py`; no name = heap), and the HUD elements (`Scene::hud`, drawn by the native side since 2026-10-16 and compared like the other objects) also come out in the line `inv HUD: ... por id (id:tris)` (by id): in the fight, portrait, health and energy bars are id `0x1f47`. It is the list of what is missing or extra in the port, generated instead of remembered. Limit: the reference comes from the emulated VU1/GS; a bug of theirs shows up as a difference (that was the case of particle 0 of the debris, a bug in the runtime's VU1, fixed on 2026-10-16).
- **Photos (2026-10-16, `native/runtime/wotm_foto.inc`, `WOTM_FOTOS`; checkbox in the launcher):** so the owner can show a bug at the moment he sees it. **F7** pauses (the EE thread stops at the capture point, right after `wotmCaptureFrame`: the finished frame stays on screen and the next one does not start; movie and sound continue), **F6** advances one frame, **F8** takes the photo: the game runs until the check records a whole frame (from `hier(0,0)` to the next), stops on it and opens a text box; Enter saves the note. The photo is the frame **after** the one that was on screen at F8 (the screen shows it before the box). Output in `<runtime>\fotos\<AAAA-MM-DD_HHMMSS>\`: `foto_NNN.vu1snap` (~43 MB, its own file, starts with record `A`), `foto_NNN_tela.png` (the native frame as it was in the window, with the hooks' fades, which the replay does not run) and `notas.txt` (notes: number, `g_frame`, `currScreen`, loaded resources and the note). To investigate: `tools\vu1cmp.ps1 <pasta>` redoes the check and the images of all photos. The reference is still the runtime's VU1/GS: for "how it looks in retail", compare with PCSX2. Pause does not become `dt`: the frame with a stop counts 1 field (`wotm_overrides.cpp`) and the scheduler pushes the vsync deadlines by the paused time (`EeScheduler::shiftHostDeadlines`, in the patch; before the fix, returning from a 14 s pause gave ~140 frames of 7 fields); the watchdog ignores the pause. Checked live: 3 F6 = 3 frames, `vu1cmp` of the photo with 99.9% matched. Without `hier(0,0)` (movie, loading) the photo gives up after 3 s. Delete resolved photos.
- **Triggers for menus:** `WOTM_VU1CHECK_WHEN=mem:<endereco>[=<valor>]` (without a value: fires on every change of the word; `mem:0x6F8464` = one frame per menu screen, `currScreen`) and the **F8** key in the window (one frame right away, with no condition or cooldown).
- **Per-pixel diff with owner (day 3, 2026-10-16, `inv px` lines, only in the replay with images):** an owner map per pixel for each side (640x224, the last one drawn wins, no Z): reference with the PATH3 clear, all the XGKICK triangles and SPRITEs in the screen buffer (whole or not) and PATH2; native with the projected objects (including sky/icons, marked), the particles, the HUD on top (`HUD <id>`) and the text. Each pixel that differs becomes a pair `ref <dono> | nat <dono>` (owner) that is counted; `nenhum` (none) = background, clear or previous framebuffer. Example (character select): `ref pkt a1e080 tex 174 | nat nenhum: 11029 px`.

It is not a bug list: it is the test sample, and its numbers serve to compare before and after. - **False alarms (the check flags them, but they are not port bugs):**
  - **HUD:** done. Both sides draw the same texture (`ref HUD 1f47 | nat HUD 1f47` in `luta_hud`); only a slight blur at the edges remains, from the way the texture is smoothed.

<summary>Particle microprograms</summary>

Read in `build/vu1/particle.txt`; the sprine was checked against the packet in a dump (`build/dumps_live/ram_B1.bin`, fx 82). Common context (qwords 0–7, from 0x256B90): q0–q3 = `worldToScreenMat` transposed; q4 = ambient×0.3, `fogFA` in w; q5 = half the focal length (192, 90.24), `fogFB` in w; q6 = (−2048, −2048, −2049, 2048); q7 = (4095, 4095, 16777215, 255). CLIP: x and y in [0, 4096], W ≥ 1, and it disappears if F < 1.

VU generator: `RINIT` does R = 0x3F800000 | (bits(fs) & 0x7FFFFF); `RNEXT` does R = (((R<<1) ^ bit4(R) ^ bit22(R)) & 0x7FFFFF) | 0x3F800000 (float in [1, 2)).

**Sprine (type 8, entry 0x2770):**
- Memory: `pos[]` at qword 8; 32 colors in 264–295; UV in 296–299; tag at 300 (pa = `preAgeFactor` in x); parameters in 301–304; M2 = row 2 of `ParticleToEmitMat` (fx+0x2CD0) at 305; 306 = ((n−1)·N, age[n−1]·N, n−1). In the packet (RAM), q300–q306 are an UNPACK of 7 qwords with the data at `pos` − 0x74 (`sprinePacketBuild` rewrites 305 and 306), right before the UNPACK of `pos` (code at `pos` − 4); before them, UV in V4-16 (USN, 1/16 of a texel; data at `pos` − 0x98) and colors in V4-8 (USN; data at `pos` − 0x11C). q302.y–w and q306.x/z are integers.
- N = max(2, int(rate@0x2D8C + 0.5)), h = 1/N. q301 = (h·(numColors−1)/lifetime, h·expansionRate, -, f6), f6 = (max−min)/min if min > 0, otherwise 1. q302 = (minSize, N, (N+1)>>1, sprites per batch). q303 = h²·(0.75−0.5N, −1.75, 0.5N+1.25, −0.25); q304 = h²·(1.5, −3.5, 2.5, −0.5).
- Curve from `pos[n−1]` to `pos[0]`, one stretch per pair A = pos[i], B = pos[i−1] (Pm before, Pn after): two parabolas with C1 in the middle. Ghosts: Pm = B − 2·M2·((B−A)·M2) at the start (reflection of B in the plane through A normal to M2; the factor 2 comes from the MADDAw with vf16.w = 2), Pn = 2·pos[0] − pos[1] at the end.
- Forward differences (bit-exact): D1 = q303·(Pm,A,B,Pn), D2 = q304·(…), D2′ = q304 reversed (w,z,y,x)·(…). p = A; per step k: emits at p, p += D1, D1 += D2; after that, when N−1−k == (N+1)>>1 (and N > 2), D1 = D1 − c·D2 + c·D2′ and D2 = D2′ (c = 0.5 with even N, 0.125 odd; the drift with odd N is retail's). Each stretch restarts at pos[i]; pos[0] is not emitted: (n−1)·N sprites.
- Per global step s: a = age[n−1] + s/N; colorPos = min(31, (numColors−1)·a/lifetime·j), j = 1 + pa·(2 − r); color = lerp of the table, +0.5, trunc (on the 1st step of a stretch that does not start a batch, j enters twice). Side = (minSize + expansionRate·a)·(1 + (r−1)·f6), screen-aligned square. F = min(fogFA + fogFB/W, 255) on the 2nd vertex.
- R: RINIT(pos[n−1].w) and one RNEXT at the start; RINIT(pos[i].w) per stretch (it only restarts the generator: the current r, vf13.w, carries on); one RNEXT per step. The UV of step k uses r_{k+1} (sel = bits & 3: 0 mirrors U and V, 1 U, 2 V, 3 normal; du′ = du − 8, dv′ = dv − 8); j and side use r_k.
- Corners: center ± q5.xy·side/W = ± (192, 90.24)·side/W px, that is, half-side side/2 in the view plane; the 1st corner takes (u0, v0), the 2nd (u1, v1).
- GIF: PRIM 0x176 (SPRITE, TME, FGE, ABE, FST), REGS = RGBAQ, UV, XYZF2, UV, XYZF2; buffers 300 and 662.

**Electric arc (types 7 and 17, entry 0x19F8; `electricArcPacketInit/Build/Update` in `asm/common/partdraw.s`):**
- fx+0x2DC0 = seed, fx+0x2D9C = `normalScale` (amplitude), fx+0x2E50 = `segmentInitU` (texture phase in U).
- Memory: `pos[]` in chains at qword 8 (head: w = 1 + number of links; link: w = 5<<(level−1) or 0; 0 in the head ends it); 16 colors (float 0..255) in 138–153; tag at 154 (PRIM 0x7C: TRISTRIP, IIP, TME, FGE, ABE, STQ); 155 = (minSize, seed, segInitU, 138 + CLUToffset); 156 = (expansionRate, jag, segDeltaU, numColors−1). Requires n ≥ 3.
- Header at `pos − 0x28` (minSize, seed, segInitU, 138 + CLUToffset, expansionRate, jag, segDeltaU, numColors − 1) and colors at `pos − 0x13C`; the low w (16 bits, ILW) of the head is 1 + number of links, the link's is 5<<(L−1) (b is 2·w qwords from a, step 5 per point).
- RINIT(seed) per packet (R carries on between the chains). A link from a to b with level L > 0 becomes 2^L sub-stretches: amp = jag·|b − a| at the 1st level, half at each level; each gap gets the midpoint 0.5a + r·amp − 1.5·amp + 0.5b, with r = (rx, ry, rz): three RNEXT on entering the link, and after each midpoint rx = ry, ry = rz, rz = RNEXT.
- Ribbon (subroutine 0x1F70), in isotropic screen space with y down (y times fxh/fyh): ghosts P₋₁ = 2P₀ − P₁ and Pₙ = 2Pₙ₋₁ − Pₙ₋₂ (homogeneous); t_in and t_out unit; n = (t.y, −t.x); v = n_in + n_out + (t_out − t_in) if t_in × t_out < 0, otherwise + (t_in − t_out), without renormalizing. Vertices S ± v·(fxh·s_k/W, fyh·s_k/W) with fxh, fyh = half the focal length (q5): in the view plane, p ± (s_k/2)·v. s_k = minSize + exp·k/S; A (+) with V = 1, B (−) with V = 0; ST = (U_k/W, 1/W or 0, 1/W), U_k = segInitU + segDeltaU·(k − S/2); color = lerp in the table (clut + k·(nc − 1)/S, + 0.5, truncated); F = fogFA + fogFB/W. ADC outside the band, with W < 1 or F < 1; 0x2330/0x2390 handle a tip with W < 1 (the tangent goes up to the plane W = 0).

**Chain (type 12, entry 0x1D00, Ultra-V's rope; resolved on 2026-10-19 with the VU1 trace):**
- Same ribbon 0x1F70, without subdivision. The traversal order comes from W(P₀) − W(Pₙ₋₁) (the MSUBz.w at the end of the header; the translation cancels out): negative, from Pₙ₋₁ to P₀ (LQD); otherwise, from P₀ to Pₙ₋₁. It starts at the farther end.
- At the tip of the rope several points coincide. The tangent between them is the zero vector: the RSQRT of 0 gives Q = maximum and 0 · max = 0. The product t_in × t_out comes out +0, "not negative", and v = n + (t_in − t_out). On the native side (y up) that is the cross < 0 branch when the order is the same and the other one when it is reversed (`rev` in `Particles`).
- The old "missing/extra" of the ropes had two causes. The first was the reference VU1 itself: compiled with `/fp:fast /arch:AVX2`, MADDq became FMA, and `vf3·q − round(vf3·q)` left 1 ulp of noise in the zero tangent, which once normalized drew a crooked ghost link. The `ps2_vu1_*.cpp` now compile with `/fp:precise`, like the native VU0 (seam in the patch's CMake). The second was the zero-product branch on the native side.

- **Kineticlops double shadow (fixed on 2026-10-19, photo `2026-10-19_142034`/001, SANFRAN):** the eye's shadow appeared twice, as if it had two eyes. Now each `drawList` item keeps the traversal's cs (`DrawItem::cs`) and `drawShadows` only projects onto items with the blocker's cs (`ShadowBlocker::shadowCs`). The quad is only in front of the floor because of its `zBufferFudge` (1.0122), so receivers with their own cs use the fudge. On the stage floor layers (fudge 1.0012/1.0024) the fudge darkened Congar's shadow beyond the reference (`congar_olhos` 50,150 → 53,354 px), so it stays only on those with their own cs. Some blockers with their own cs (monsters in split screen and in `shell_logo`) are left without a receiver in the frame; per the asm, retail does the same. - **Alpha above 1 in the common blend (fixed on 2026-10-19):** the GS lets As go above 1 (At·Av/128 up to 255; ~300 objects per frame with As ≈ 1.03, texture 0xFF and vertex 0x42, and a few with As ≈ 2), and in the common blend it does Cs·As − Cd·(As − 1), clamped to 0..255. The native side clamped As to 1. Now the objects where As can go above 1 (the highest vertex alpha times the highest of the texture, `MeshData::maxA` and `texAmax_`) come out in three passes of the same geometry, each pixel deciding in the shader (modes 3 to 5): the common blend with alpha 0 where As > 1; `Cs − Cd·(1 − 1/As)` only where As > 1 (`gsBlendOver1`, subtraction); and the destination times As (`gsBlendOver2`, output As − 1 times `DST_COLOR`, plus the destination). The 8-bit framebuffer only rounds result/As (error < 1). The sky dome is left out: the reference does not show the gain on it (the destination under the dome is the previous frame, the same sky, and the math converges to Cs), and on the native side, over the black clear, it came out ×1.027. The 2026-10-16 attempt (reverted) looked worse because the native side was ~2 levels brighter than the reference over the whole frame (GS math in float in the shader; fixed on 2026-10-19, `WOTM_GSTRUNC`): without discounting that, brightening an object always increases the error.
